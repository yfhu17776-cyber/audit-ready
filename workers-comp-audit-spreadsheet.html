InsureRecon

Workers' Comp Audit Spreadsheet Alternative

A browser-based alternative to manually reconciling Workers' Comp audit spreadsheets, subcontractor payments and insurance certificates.

The problem

Spreadsheets are useful for storing records, but manual reconciliation becomes painful when payment rows, vendor names, certificates and renewal dates live in different places.

How the workflow works

InsureRecon is designed to perform the repetitive comparison: payment → vendor → evidence → coverage date → exception → next action.

What you get

Keep your source records and use the tool to identify what needs attention. Uncertain evidence dates stay in MANUAL REVIEW rather than being silently treated as confirmed.
