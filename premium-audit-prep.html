Workers' Comp Premium Audit Preparation

Before an audit, contractors can spend hours rebuilding the same trail from AP exports, spreadsheets, certificates and follow-up notes. A useful preparation workflow should turn those records into a targeted exception list.

What to prepare

  • Audit start and end dates
  • Subcontractor payment/AP records
  • Workers' Comp evidence and renewals
  • Self-insurance or exemption records when applicable
  • Source filenames and policy identifiers

What to look for

  • Payments with no matching evidence
  • Evidence for the wrong coverage type
  • Payments falling into coverage gaps
  • Evidence outside the audit period
  • Dates inferred from ambiguous document text

Reduce the manual review list

InsureRecon performs the payment-to-evidence comparison locally in the browser and keeps uncertain cases in MANUAL REVIEW. The goal is not to replace a carrier, broker or auditor; it is to reduce the time spent finding which records need attention.
