The problem

If your audit preparation starts with an AP export and a folder full of certificates, the hard part is reconciling the two. InsureRecon is built for that specific job.

How the workflow works

Import payment/AP records, import evidence, match vendors conservatively, compare payment dates with evidence coverage, then export only the records that need attention.

What you get

Unlike a simple certificate expiration list, the workflow starts with actual payments. That makes coverage gaps, missing records and wrong evidence types visible against the records that matter for audit preparation.
