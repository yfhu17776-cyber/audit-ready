InsureRecon

Subcontractor Payment Insurance Reconciliation

Reconcile subcontractor payments with Workers' Comp insurance evidence and find missing records, wrong evidence and coverage gaps.

The problem

A subcontractor can have a certificate on file and still require review. The useful question is whether the evidence lines up with the actual payment record and date.

How the workflow works

Upload the payment/AP export and evidence files. InsureRecon matches vendor names conservatively, checks evidence type and coverage dates, and separates confirmed matches from exceptions and manual review.

What you get

The output is designed to reduce follow-up work: review list, evidence map, evidence requests and an audit preparation report.
