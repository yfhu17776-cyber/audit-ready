Workers' Comp Audit Checklist for Contractors

A practical checklist for construction contractors preparing a Workers' Comp premium/payroll audit.

1. Gather payment/AP records

  • Export subcontractor/vendor payments for the audit period.
  • Keep vendor names, payment dates and amounts.
  • Do not rely only on a current vendor list; reconcile against actual payments.

2. Gather insurance evidence

  • Collect Workers' Comp certificates/policies and renewals.
  • Collect self-insurance or exemption records when applicable.
  • Keep the original source filename and document dates.

3. Check the evidence chain

  • Match each paid vendor to the correct evidence.
  • Check that the evidence type is appropriate.
  • Check whether the payment date falls inside the evidence period.
  • Look for gaps between an old record and a renewal.
  • Keep uncertain dates in manual review rather than assuming coverage.

4. Build the exception list

  • Missing evidence
  • Wrong evidence type
  • Coverage gaps
  • Outside audit-period payments
  • Ambiguous document dates

5. Prepare the review package

Export the exceptions, evidence map and targeted evidence requests so the remaining work is clear.
