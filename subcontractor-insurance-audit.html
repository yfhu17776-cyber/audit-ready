Subcontractor Insurance Audit: Reconcile Payments to Evidence

The hard part of audit preparation is often not finding a certificate. It is proving that the evidence you have actually lines up with the subcontractor payments and dates in your records.

The reconciliation problem

A vendor can have an insurance document on file and still require review if the document is the wrong coverage type, starts after a payment date, ends before a payment date, or has dates that cannot be confirmed from the source.

A better workflow

  1. Import the payment/AP export.
  2. Import the evidence files.
  3. Match vendor names conservatively.
  4. Compare payment dates with evidence effective/expiration dates.
  5. Separate confirmed matches from exceptions and manual review.
  6. Export only the records that need follow-up.

Why payment dates matter

Looking only at certificate expiration dates can hide a gap between two records. Reconciliation against actual payment dates makes the missing period visible.
