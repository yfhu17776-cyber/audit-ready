Scope: AuditReady is an evidence-preparation tool for U.S. California Workers’ Comp premium/payroll audit preparation, initially focused on construction contractors using subcontractors. It does not decide legal contractor status, payroll/exposure inclusion, classification, coverage validity or final premium. A “match” is an evidence/date match in the imported records, not a legal or insurance determination.

Privacy: files are parsed locally by browser JavaScript; this page has no server upload function. Do not use it as a substitute for an insurer, auditor, accountant or attorney.
