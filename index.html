California Workers’ Comp Premium Audit Prep

Reconstruct the evidence trail behind subcontractor payments before a premium audit: match payments to Workers’ Comp evidence, detect coverage-period gaps and missing records, and export a review-ready exception report.

Exact scope

United States → California → Workers’ Compensation insurance → insurer premium/payroll audit → construction contractors using subcontractors.

What AuditReady does: maps each payment to imported evidence by subcontractor and date, then shows exactly which payments need human follow-up. What it does not decide: legal independent-contractor status, payroll/exposure inclusion, classification code, coverage validity, or final premium.

A review item means the current imported records are insufficient or ambiguous for the payment/evidence link. “Matched” means the imported records contain evidence that appears to be Workers’ Comp-related and whose dates cover the payment date. It is an evidence match, not a legal or insurance determination.

Regulatory basis: California Department of Insurance explains that final Workers’ Comp premium is determined after payroll records are audited; insurers generally have audit rights during the policy period and for three years after. WCIRB Rule 4 requires insurers to audit employer records and states that audit reports show the payroll source(s). This tool is an evidence-preparation layer only. Sources: California Department of Insurance · WCIRB USRP Rule 4.

Privacy: files are parsed locally by browser JavaScript; this page has no server upload function. Do not use it as a substitute for an insurer, auditor, accountant, or attorney.
