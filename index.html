Scope: United States → California → construction contractors/subcontractor-heavy businesses → Workers’ Comp premium/payroll audit evidence preparation. AuditReady helps organize and compare imported records; it does not decide legal contractor status, payroll/exposure inclusion, classification, coverage validity, or final premium. Evidence match ≠ legal conclusion.

Why this is different: ordinary COI products focus on collecting, grading and monitoring certificates. AuditReady focuses on reconstructing the audit evidence chain around actual payments and dates. Public market products already offer automatic ACORD reading, compliance grading and expiration monitoring, so this product deliberately does not compete as another generic COI tracker.
